Let head_loss return the summed cross entropy for a caller that divides by its own total

`head_loss` divided by the rows -- all of them, or `valid_rows` -- so a
caller accumulating several microbatches into one step had no way to weigh
every token by the step's own total; the mean of each microbatch was the
only normalization on offer, and padding rows sat in its denominator.

`head_loss(..., reduction="sum")` returns the cross entropy summed over the
rows whose target is not `-100`, on both providers: the builtin one runs
its chunk loop with a normalizer of one, and the native one skips the
division. `valid_rows` names a denominator, so it is refused beside
`"sum"`. The mean is unchanged and stays the default. The operations
document gains the argument; the test checks the sum on both providers
against the mean times the rows and against `torch.nn.functional`,
gradients included, and the refusal.
