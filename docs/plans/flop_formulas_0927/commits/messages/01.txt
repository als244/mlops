Give every operation one logical cost, and register it as its flop formula

Cost hints lived next to the providers that happened to have them: two
operations carried an estimator, the rest none, and nothing outside mlops
could ask what an operation costs. A partitioner deciding whether a value is
cheap to regenerate needs exactly that, through a channel it already reads.

`mlops.dispatch.logical_costs` now holds one estimator per operation, in the
operation's own semantic signature, returning its logical flops and bytes
for the forward and the backward entrypoints. `flop_formula(*operators)`
registers a function as the flop count of custom operators in PyTorch's flop
registry, so `torch.utils.flop_counter.FlopCounterMode` counts them like any
matrix product, and `has_flop_formula(operator)` says whether one is
registered. The provider-local estimators are gone; `estimate_implementation`
reaches the canonical ones.

The architecture, API, operations, extending and providers documents
describe the module, the two functions, and the step a new operation or
provider takes to declare its cost.
