Register a flop formula on every custom operator

Every `mlops::*` operator registered with PyTorch now has a flop formula
beside its fake, forward and backward alike, reading the canonical estimator
for its operation. Variants of one operation share one formula: the table
and analytic rotations, the Triton and legacy swiglu, the functional and
in-place AdamW forms, the fused MoE and its prepare/finish decomposition,
whose two halves add up to the fused count. The packed-sequence metadata
preparation declares zero flops, which is what it does.

The test walks `torch.ops.mlops` and fails on any operator without a
formula, checks every opaque operation has a canonical estimator, and pins
the closed forms: norms, activations, rotations, gathers, losses, attention,
the hybrid operators, MoE and AdamW. 227 passed, 4 skipped, in the full
suite.
